Inside the four weeks

What you'll walk away with

This is the group version of my one on one coaching: the same transformation, made more accessible, and richer because we learn from each other. Every session is live, practical, and built around your real hard moments.

✦

The family centered framework

The paradigm shift underneath everything else: moving from the child centered default, where the whole home orbits around managing kids, to leading as a family unit. You'll get clear on the values you're instilling and what you're actually building toward.

✦

Practical tools for the hard moments

Word for word tools for tantrums, defiance, not listening, and sibling conflict, practiced out loud on your real scenarios so they're in your hands when the moment hits.

✦

Tools for you, not just your kids

How to be the calm, regulated parent even in the hardest moments. The parent you always pictured being is still in there, and these tools make that version of you accessible when you're currently stuck in reaction and overwhelm.

✦

A room of parents in it with you

Real community, not a lecture. A space to talk through the hard parenting moments with people who get it, get on the same page as your spouse, and land on real solutions that create lasting change.

✦

A workbook you keep forever

Your own take-home workbook, packed with value, to use in class and long after. Something real to work through at home, even with a spouse who couldn't be in the room.

Questions you might have

What ages is this for?

The tools land best for parents of kids roughly 2 to 10, but the leadership framework works at any age. If you're not sure it fits your family, text me and ask.

What if I miss a week?

Life happens. You'll get the week's core material and your one shift to practice, and I'll catch you up so you don't fall behind the group.

Can my spouse come too?

Yes, please. One price covers your family, and this works fastest when both parents are running the same playbook.

Do I need to bring my kids?

No, this is for the grown-ups. Consider it a Tuesday evening that pays your whole family back.

Is this gentle parenting?

No, and it's not old-school authoritarian either. Gentle parenting asks you to defer. Authoritarian parenting asks you to control. I teach you to lead: warm, calm, and firmly in charge.
